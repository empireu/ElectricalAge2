# Crusher:
### Sounds:

- https://sounds.deadsounds.com/grinding-sound-effects
- https://sounds.deadsounds.com/stones-rocks-sound-effects

How: I took stone sounds, added the grinding sound on top, then cut a part of the front of the clip and pasted it somewhere at the end such that half was intersecting the end and half was past the end, then crossfaded the intersected region.
